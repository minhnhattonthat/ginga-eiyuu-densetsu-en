# Synopsis - Ginga Eiyuu Densetsu (SNES, 1992)

A fleet-battle strategy game following Yoshiki Tanaka's novels (and the anime) from Astarte to the Baalat Treaty.
The player picks Yang (Free Planets Alliance) or Reinhard (Galactic Empire) and fights each story battle in order;
narration between battles tells the history, short scenes play the famous moments. Results text depends on
win/loss. Names and terms: `glossary.md`.

## Who is who
- **Reinhard von Lohengramm** (born von Musel): young Imperial admiral; the Kaiser Friedrich IV took his sister
  **Annerose** as a concubine; he swears to bring down the Goldenbaum dynasty and take the throne. **Siegfried
  Kircheis**, his childhood friend and right hand, calls him "Reinhard-sama". **Oberstein**: cold, ruthless chief of
  staff. Admirals: Mittermeyer, Reuenthal, Bittenfeld, Muller, Kempf, Wahlen, Lutz, Kessler, Mecklinger, Eisenach,
  Fahrenheit, Steinmetz, Lennenkamp. **Hilda** (Hildegard von Mariendorf): sharp noblewoman who sides with Reinhard.
- **Yang Wen-li**: Alliance officer who would rather be a historian; lazy, wry, brilliant ("Miracle Yang", "Magician").
  Hero of El Facil (evacuated the civilians when Admiral Lynch fled). **Frederica Greenhill**: his aide (her father
  Dwight Greenhill leads the coup); **Julian Mintz**: his ward; **Cazerne**: logistics; **Attenborough**,
  **Fischer**, **Merkatz** (Imperial admiral who defects to Yang with his aide **Schneider**), **Schonkopf** (Rosen
  Ritter commander), **Lappe** (Yang's friend, survives Astarte here), **Bucock** (old admiral). **Paetta**: pompous
  commander at Astarte. **Trunicht**: hypocritical Alliance politician.
- **Phezzan**: neutral trading dominion between the two; **Rubinsky** (Landesherr) schemes with both; **Rupert
  Kesselring** his aide.

## Story order (S.E. = Alliance calendar, I.C. = Imperial; S.E. 796 = I.C. 487)
1. Prologue: humanity leaves Earth; Rudolf von Goldenbaum founds the Empire; Heinessen leads the Long March and
   founds the Alliance; first battle S.E. 640 (Dagon). The heroes appear.
2. Battle of Astarte (S.E. 796): Reinhard's 20,000 ships against three Alliance fleets of 40,000; Paetta is wounded
   and Yang takes command, saving the rest.
3. Yang takes Iserlohn Fortress with the Rosen Ritter (Schonkopf) in disguise; Frederica becomes his aide.
4. Alliance invasion of the Empire ends at Amritsar (Kircheis's flank attack). Reinhard made Fleet Admiral / Marquis.
5. Friedrich IV dies; the child Erwin Josef II is enthroned; Reinhard allies with Lichtenlade. The great nobles
   (Braunschweig, Littenheim) form the Lippstadt Pact: civil war. At the same time, Reinhard's agents incite the
   Military Congress for the Salvation of the Republic coup on Heinessen (Greenhill); Yang crushes it.
6. Westerland: Braunschweig nukes his own planet; Reinhard lets it happen (Oberstein's advice). Geiersburg falls;
   **Ansbach** brings Braunschweig's body before Reinhard and draws a hand cannon hidden in it; **Kircheis dies
   shielding Reinhard** (late reveal: don't foreshadow it). Merkatz defects to Yang.
7. Reinhard rules as Imperial Prime Minister. Geiersburg Fortress is warped to Iserlohn (Kempf, Muller); Yang,
   recalled to a board of inquiry on Heinessen, returns and wins.
8. Erwin Josef is abducted to the Alliance (Legitimate Government of the Galactic Empire). Operation Ragnarok: the
   Empire takes Phezzan and invades. Battles of Rantemario (Bucock) and Vermillion (Yang vs Reinhard); the
   Alliance government surrenders just as Yang is about to win.
9. Baalat Treaty; Reinhard becomes Kaiser (New Imperial Calendar 1); Yang retires.
